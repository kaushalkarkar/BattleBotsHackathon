# 🎬 Video walkthrough script (~2–3 min)

A short, clear demo is a **mandatory** part of the #BattleBotsDev submission and
is judged on "Community Impact & Presentation." Record your screen showing the
live app, talk through it naturally. Keep it under ~3 minutes.

**Live app:** https://battle-bots-hackathon.vercel.app/
**Tip:** record in 1080p, speak clearly, and show the app doing real things.

---

## Suggested flow

**0:00 — Hook (15s)**
> "This is the BattleBots Win Predictor — a full-stack app that predicts who wins
> any BattleBots matchup, built for the Bright Data BattleBotsDev competition.
> All the robot data is collected with Bright Data."

**0:15 — Predictor + why it's not a black box (40s)**
- Pick two robots (e.g. Tombstone vs Minotaur), click **Predict**.
- Point out: the win probability bar, the **confidence** level, and the
  **plain-English reasons** (win rate, KO rate, weapon matchup, head-to-head).
- Show the **radar chart** comparing the two robots.
> "Every prediction is explained — win rate, finishing power, and a
> weapon-versus-weapon matchup model. No black box."

**0:55 — Robot profiles (20s)**
- Click a robot in the leaderboard → show stats + full match history, and click
  an opponent to jump to their card.

**1:15 — Tournament simulator (30s)**
- Go to the **Tournament** tab → Run tournament → show the bracket and champion.
> "It can simulate a full single-elimination bracket and predict the champion."

**1:45 — Model accuracy / rigor (30s)**
- Go to **Model accuracy** → show 66.7% on 66 real fights, and that accuracy
  rises with confidence (HIGH 80% → LOW 59%).
> "I backtested the model on 66 real fights — 66.7% accuracy, and it's more
> accurate when it's more confident. This isn't guessing."

**2:15 — Insights + Bright Data (25s)**
- Show **Insights** (weapon meta + biggest upsets).
- Show the code / mention: "The data is scraped with Bright Data's Web Unlocker;
  the backend is FastAPI, the frontend is Angular."

**2:40 — Close (10s)**
> "Code and live demo are linked in the description. Built with Bright Data.
> #BattleBotsDev."
