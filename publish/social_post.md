## LinkedIn (attach the video)
For the Bright Data #BattleBotsDev competition, I built a full-stack **BattleBots Win Predictor**. 🤖⚔️

🔹 Bright Data's Web Unlocker collects BattleBots match history, weapon specs & KO records
🔹 A FastAPI backend scores each matchup on form, finishing power & weapon advantage
🔹 An Angular dashboard: predictions with a radar comparison, robot profiles, a tournament bracket simulator, weapon meta analysis, a live leaderboard, and dark/light themes
🔹 Backtested on 66 real fights → 66.7% accuracy (vs 50% coin-flip), and confidence correlates with accuracy (HIGH 80% → LOW 59%)

No black box — every prediction is explained.

Stack: Angular 17 · FastAPI · pandas · Bright Data
